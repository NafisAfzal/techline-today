## Governance proposals, pending owner review

For Kubernetes, require specific-item evidence that establishes original creator/origin, actual license scope, exception checks and feasible attribution. Hold unlabeled guests, mirrors, syndication and ambiguous original links. A site footer or repository license alone is not an automated item decision. Do not assume general owner authorization accepts publisher license terms.

For KEV, review the database/item scope and third-party exceptions separately from a linked vendor advisory. Keep catalog-addition and applicable remediation dates at day precision. Do not promote an entire catalog into breaking news or copy third-party reference prose.

For all sources, preserve affirmative evidence for every requested operation: storing metadata, republishing title/excerpt, summarizing, translation, archival and AI submission. The operator's proposed permitted-fields list remains inert until a future separately reviewed approval pathway exists. Unknown or expired evidence must deny.
