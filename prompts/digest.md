# Digest Prompt Contract

Input consists only of already validated StoryCluster records.

Create a concise digest with:
- top five stories;
- one sentence connecting the day's/week's most important theme if evidence supports it;
- notable AI/model release;
- notable developer/API/freebie item;
- material chip/device item if available;
- `watch next` limited to explicitly announced future events/dates.

Do not predict unannounced launches or market outcomes.

Use plain international English, short sentences and natural wording. Explain the change before its significance. Preserve technical names, attribution and uncertainty. Avoid corporate language, hype and filler. The editorial style contract is docs/EDITORIAL_STYLE.md.
