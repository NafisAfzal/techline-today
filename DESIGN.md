# Design System: Techline Today

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The technology news desk"**

A recognizable publication built around readable reporting, restrained structure, and useful evidence. Navy lettering anchors the identity, blue identifies interaction, and a small teal second voice distinguishes the masthead. Warm reading surfaces, a cool topic directory, and a navy utility field give each kind of content a clear place. Headlines and source information carry the page.

The system is editorial and compact, with a strong system-sans masthead and English section titles, serif reporting headlines, plain controls, thin rules, and mostly flat surfaces. The working name remains provisional; its lettering and protected English presentation describe the current artifact, not legal clearance. The owner-directed homepage composition lives in its surface brief rather than becoming a template for every page.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Strong sans-serif publication lettering with serif reporting and plain, readable controls.
- Warm light surfaces and layered navy dark surfaces.
- Clear distinctions between a topic directory, editorial columns, and a navy tools field.
- Visible evidence, source links, and meaningful unknowns.
- Observable, controlled playback in a stable headline slot.

## Colors

The frontmatter names match the CSS semantic roles in [global.css](apps/web/src/styles/global.css); `dialog-backdrop` records the native search backdrop. Unsuffixed values are warm/light; corresponding `-dark` values replace the same CSS properties when `data-theme="dark"` is present. The directory, editorial and tools headings have their own semantic accents. Utility ink, supporting ink, links, teal, gold and dividers are independently selected in each appearance. Components share structure across themes. The sidecar's synthesized tonal ramps are historical swatch previews, not additional production colors.

### Primary

- **Midnight masthead ink** (`navy`): wordmark, two-page folio emblem, and the header's top rule; dark appearance uses light publication ink.
- **Editorial blue** (`accent`): actionable links, current navigation, focus outlines, caret, selection, source-panel emphasis, and the leading edge of the lead story.

### Secondary

- **Publication teal** (`brand-secondary`): the second wordmark color, the folio accent, significance labels, and translation-note detail.
- **Quiet indigo** (`indigo`): one stage of the timed In focus progress accent. It is not a headline or canvas color.
- **Directory teal** (`section-category`): topic-directory heading; deep teal on the cool light field, soft teal on navy.
- **Editorial blue** (`section-stories`): further-news heading and its shared upper rule; deep editorial blue in warm appearance, pale blue in dark.
- **Utility azure** (`section-tools`): the tools section heading on its navy field; distinct from both reading ink and the gold/teal module labels.

### Tertiary

- **Credit gold** (`warm`): the light-appearance credit role outside the dark utility field; the corresponding dark value retains that meaning.
- **Evidence green** (`green`): primary-confirmed and multi-source evidence.
- **Caution amber** (`amber`): verification caution, limited evidence, and expiring states.
- **Expiry rose** (`coral`): expiry semantics.

Credit bronze and caution amber use distinct light shades and share their dark shade while remaining separate roles.

### Neutral

- **Warm canvas** (`bg`), **reading paper** (`surface`), **cool grouped field** (`secondary`), and **summary field** (`featured`) distinguish page, reporting, selected controls, and article summary.
- **Topic directory** (`directory-bg`) separates topic navigation from reporting; its dark counterpart remains a cool navy field.
- **Header field** (`header-bg`) is soft editorial blue in light appearance and the existing raised navy in dark. Light summary/ticker blue, directory mint and warm reading paper give surfaces distinct roles.
- **Utility navy** (`utility-bg`) groups tools, with **utility ink** (`utility-text`), **utility supporting ink** (`utility-muted`), **utility links** (`utility-link`), and **utility dividers** (`utility-line`) selected for contrast on that field.
- **Utility teal** (`utility-teal`) names model tools; **utility gold** (`utility-gold`) names credit tools. Their shades are independently tuned for each navy background.
- **Search backdrop** (`dialog-backdrop`) dims the reading canvas while the native dialog is open.
- **Strong ink** (`text`) and **secondary ink** (`muted`) establish reading hierarchy.
- **Rule** (`line`) and **soft rule** (`soft-line`) separate structural regions and supporting details.

**The Semantic Accent Rule.** Keep blue for interaction, teal for the identity's second voice, and status colors attached to their meaning. Do not reuse expiry rose as masthead color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Georgia with Times New Roman and serif fallbacks for general page and article titles. Story headlines use Georgia with a serif fallback.
**Publication and Section Font:** the platform system stack for the protected wordmark, compact homepage title, and English section headings.
**Body Font:** the platform system stack recorded in the frontmatter.
**Data Font:** ui-monospace for factual values and appropriate measurements; meaningful monospace source labels use the recorded SFMono-Regular and Consolas fallbacks.
**Bangla Font:** Nirmala UI, Vrinda, Noto Sans Bengali, Hind Siliguri, Lohit Bengali, then sans-serif.

The serif hierarchy gives reporting a publication voice; the bold sans-serif masthead and English section headings give the portal clear structure. Controls and metadata stay direct. These are the actual installed-font stacks, with no webfont download.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** general page and article titles use the fluid display role; article titles tighten line height to 1.18 and balance within 32ch.
- **Wordmark:** the 44px dedicated wordmark role reduces to 36px at 1100px, the recorded wordmark-phone role at 720px, and 27px at 380px. The separate footer-wordmark role stays at 26px at every breakpoint.
- **Lead headline:** the headline role reduces to the recorded headline-phone role at 720px and 27px at 380px; the lead measure is 28ch.
- **Title:** standard feed headlines use the title role. Briefing headlines use the compact briefing-headline role; its section heading uses briefing-section. Further-story columns use 24px/1.25 headlines, reduced to 23px at 720px. English section headings use the sans-serif section role.
- **Homepage title:** the compact homepage role reduces to 25px on phones. It is not the general display scale.
- **Major homepage sections:** only Explore by category, More stories and Tools & trackers use the homepage-major-section role: 700/30px, 28px at 1100px, 26px at 720px. English line height is 1.2 with -0.02em tracking; Bangla uses 1.5 with normal tracking. Shared 25px section headings and the 23px briefing title retain their size.
- **Body and prose:** body uses the recorded body role; article and methodology prose use the prose role with a 66ch measure.
- **Summary:** feed previews use the summary role; the lead uses 18px with 1.65 line height.
- **Label:** factual metadata uses the label role. Edition, focus-support, and briefing metadata use the compact micro treatment; action links use 15px. Existing source-type labels retain the data-label role for semantic content.

Bangla retains the existing script-specific font architecture: body line height is 1.8, heading treatment uses 1.5 with normal tracking, and story prose and summaries use 1.85. Explicit English reporting headings retain the serif stack; the protected English wordmark, homepage title, and section headings use their system-sans roles. Numbers in timestamps and fact chips use tabular figures.

**The Reading Measure Rule.** Keep prose at the existing 66ch measure and preserve script-specific line height; do not squeeze Bangla copy into English leading.

## Layout

The shared centered container reaches 1240px with 32px desktop side gutters. Gutters reduce to 20px at 1000px and 16px at 720px. Safe-area insets remain part of the page edges.

Spacing follows the recorded four/eight-pixel rhythm. Shared controls and their labels form tight groups; reporting regions use larger 24–40px separation. Standard feed cards use 24px padding, phone cards 20px, and compact density 16px. The homepage lead uses 24px 28px 20px, phone 20px, and narrow-phone 18px padding.

The homepage's current lead/brief split is 1.4:1 with a 32px gap. At 1100px it stacks, while the compact four-story brief becomes two columns; at 720px the brief is a single list. The cool topic directory uses three columns, two at 720px, and one at 380px. Further stories keep three editorial columns until 720px, then become a single list with shared horizontal rules. The navy tools band uses 32px padding and two modules with a shared divider; at 720px it uses 24px 20px padding and stacked modules. This composition is described by the [homepage surface brief](.impeccable/surfaces/homepage.md).

Directory rows have zero inline inset, aligning the icon edges, descriptions and shared rules with their heading. The More stories action aligns to the heading row instead of the combined heading/description block. Desktop tracker descriptions reserve two lines so field labels and actions align across both modules; phones retain natural text flow. Heading-to-content gaps stay 20px, section separation stays 40px/32px and mobile gutters stay 16px.

Article detail uses a 2:1 reading/source split with a 40px gap, then one column at 720px. Tracker records use a heading/body grid, also stacking at 720px. Phone article actions remain sticky and source targets account for their height.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat reading surfaces, tonal fields, and thin rules carry the page. Story cards and category rows do not lift or cast shadows on hover; their rules and links clarify interaction. The native search dialog is the one elevated reading tool, using the light/dark `--shadow` values and a dark translucent backdrop. Exact shadow values live in the sidecar because they fall outside the frontmatter schema.

**The Flat Reporting Rule.** Keep reporting and navigation surfaces flat. Use the existing dialog shadow for protected search focus.

## Shapes

Controls and the In focus strip have gently eased corners using the control radius. Topic-directory rows are square and unboxed. The appearance group uses its slightly larger outer radius around the two control-radius choices. Search uses the dialog radius; keyboard hints use the keyboard-key radius. Story, source, and tracker records retain square corners. Borders are thin semantic rules rather than decorative frames inside frames.

The homepage tools field is an unframed navy band. Its two modules share a vertical utility divider on desktop and a horizontal divider when stacked on phones; they do not introduce nested cards. Further-story columns likewise use shared rules instead of individual frames. These distinctions belong to the current patterns rather than a universal ban on borders.

## Components

### Buttons

Plain outlined actions use the recorded default button primitive, with a minimum 44px height and width. Hover changes the border and text to blue; pressed state uses the grouped neutral field and blue outline. Disabled controls halve opacity. Keyboard focus uses a 3px blue outline with a 3px offset. Pointer press scales to 0.97 over 160ms with the recorded exponential ease-out; keyboard focus stays stable and reduced motion removes animation.

### Chips and evidence

Fact chips use a cool neutral fill, soft border, compact padding, a blue monospace value, and a muted sans-serif label. Story previews use meaningfully labelled source-reported numbers. Lead and feed previews filter out values whose canonical label is exactly `Source-reported` before limiting the display to one or two eligible values respectively. Meaningful translated labels remain aligned with their canonical entries by index. Original values and the complete numeric evidence remain available on detail pages. Evidence links remain readable text with a small outlined dot, semantic status color, underline, and a 44px target. Tracker badges retain factual status. Provider/source metadata remains meaningful content, not a reusable decorative eyebrow pattern.

### Cards and containers

Standard story previews combine metadata, a serif headline, summary, optional source-backed facts, original-source/details links, and a local Save action. The footer is separated by a soft rule, and status feedback stays beside the action. The lead can include up to two existing summary bullets when an original-source excerpt supports them, in addition to its preview and significance text. The homepage's further-story columns align their action rows while allowing natural text height.

Topic-directory rows are navigation: an authored topic icon, category, description, actual count, and Browse action. They contain no article headline. Fine-pointer hover changes the row field to the summary neutral over 160ms with standard ease; its shared rule stays structural, and reduced motion removes the transition. Tools & trackers uses the distinct navy band described above with clear field labels and tracker links. It remains available even when offers or model records are absent; destination routes retain honest empty states. The band does not duplicate a current offer or model teaser. Full tracker records use a square reading surface and labelled data.

### Inputs and search

Fields use the reading surface, thin rule, control radius, blue caret, and the shared focus outline. Labels stay visible; placeholder text uses secondary ink. Search opens a native dialog with a bounded 660px width, 85dvh maximum height, and 28px padding, reduced to 16px on phones. Escape and backdrop activation close it; focus stays inside and returns to the trigger. Results use natural rows, blue links, and a neutral hover/focus field. Queries use the existing 120ms debounce and stale-result protection.

The desktop masthead exposes a real search form, `clamp(280px,24vw,340px)` wide above 1000px. Its localized input shares the existing query state; Enter or the magnifier opens the existing dialog with the typed query. Escape restores the originating input/button. The form owns one 3px keyboard outline. At 1000px and below the same submit button becomes the compact search action; mobile keeps its text above 380px. No additional search island, index request on header typing, or dependency is introduced.

### Navigation and appearance

Primary navigation uses plain labels and a blue current-page underline. Fine-pointer hover reveals the same underline; keyboard focus remains immediate. More discloses a bounded menu instead of pushing the navigation beyond narrow screens. Below 380px Chips moves into More; Bangla also moves Security there. Bangla phone layouts move Chips into More below 720px. Each destination has only one visible link, preserving a single navigation row and 44px targets. The language selector uses EN / বাংলা links with a visible selected state.

Appearance presents two explicit segmented Light/Dark buttons. Both labels remain visible in each locale and at narrow widths. The visible Appearance caption is removed; its localized accessible group name remains. Drawn sun/moon icons remain visible through the phone layout and are omitted at 380px and below. The selected choice uses blue fill and canvas-colored text, and exactly one button has `aria-pressed="true"`. Selection updates theme metadata and local preferences; unavailable storage reports that the change applies to this visit. Settings also offers Follow device.

### Wordmark

The protected English wordmark pairs navy Techline with teal Today and an authored two-page folio SVG. Its descriptor sits beneath the lettering. The system-sans lettering uses the dedicated 750-weight role and responsive sizes recorded above. Keep `lang="en"`, `translate="no"`, and `notranslate` semantics; the masthead does not imply naming clearance.

### In focus

The strip rotates up to four actual ranked headlines every eight seconds and shows a localized position such as 1 / 4. Every item remains available in the stationary lead/brief. Desktop uses ellipsis while retaining the full title; phones reserve a two-line 52px headline slot. When the headline link has visible keyboard focus, its clipping wrapper also draws the shared 3px blue outline with a 3px offset, keeping the focus indicator visible without changing headline geometry. Automatic headline changes use a 220ms exponential ease-out from 0.85 opacity to full opacity, with no translation. Requested Previous/Next changes appear immediately.

Previous, Pause/Resume, and Next controls accompany a visible Playing/Paused state. A thin two-pixel progress meter fills over the same eight-second interval while stepping through blue, teal, and indigo; it pauses with the timer and restarts for the next headline. Pausing preserves the remaining interval. Pointer hover over the label or content pauses reading; playback controls remain usable, so Resume works while the pointer remains over its button; visible keyboard focus anywhere in the strip, a hidden tab, offscreen placement, or an open native search dialog also pauses playback. Closing search respects manual pause and the remaining interval.

Reduced motion shows Motion off, stops automatic rotation and entrance/progress animation, and retains usable Previous/Next buttons; only requested headline changes are announced. A one-headline edition shows One headline with no navigation/playback controls or meter. Without JavaScript the first linked headline remains static and playback controls remain hidden. These behaviors are implemented by [NewsFocus.astro](apps/web/src/components/NewsFocus.astro) and [news-focus.ts](apps/web/src/lib/news-focus.ts), with localized labels from the existing locale dictionaries.

**The Stable Reading Rule.** Motion belongs in the controlled In focus slot; the original ranked stories, headline colors, facts, and numbers stay stationary.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use the same semantic CSS properties for both appearances and preserve visible current-state labels.
- **Do** retain the 66ch prose measure, script-aware font stacks, and explicit English fallback.
- **Do** distinguish navigation, story previews, and source-backed tool records.
- **Do** keep keyboard focus, original-source links, meaningful status metadata, and action feedback visible.
- **Do** give rotating headlines a stationary alternative, visible playback state, and Previous/Pause/Next controls; preserve remaining time on pause and manual navigation under reduced motion.

### Don't:

- **Don't** add gradients, glow, promotional imagery, or rose-led identity treatment to this publication.
- **Don't** turn provider names, evidence states, or real edition status into decorative marketing badges.
- **Don't** export inherited eyebrow styling as a decorative primitive; preserve meaningful provider, evidence, and edition metadata.
- **Don't** replace the tools band with nested rounded cards or animate the reading canvas and original facts.
- **Don't** treat the provisional brand, fixture examples, or local renders as legal clearance, production translation, or deployment evidence.
